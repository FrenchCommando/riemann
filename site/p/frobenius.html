Frobenius: the symmetries of a finite field

Galois, 1830; named after Georg Frobenius (1896)
Theorem

In a field with pnp^npn elements, the map φ(a)=ap\varphi(a) = a^pφ(a)=ap preserves +++ and ×\times×, fixes exactly the elements of Fp\mathbb F_pFp​, and has order nnn: φn\varphi^nφn is the identity and no smaller power is. If a polynomial with coefficients in Fp\mathbb F_pFp​ has a root β\betaβ, then βp,βp2,…\beta^p, \beta^{p^2}, \dotsβp,βp2,… are roots too.

Galois theory is about the symmetries of a field: the bijections that preserve +++ and ×\times×. Finite fields have the simplest kind. Every symmetry is a power of one map, raising to the ppp-th power, so the symmetry group is cyclic. That is the base case of the general theory, and it is also how the 15-bit format strip printed beside the corner squares of every QR code was designed.

Builds on Fermat's Little Theorem, Every finite field has a primitive element.

Proof · 6 steps
  1. 1The freshman's dream

    In a field of characteristic ppp, (a+b)p=ap+bp(a + b)^p = a^p + b^p(a+b)p=ap+bp and (ab)p=apbp(ab)^p = a^p b^p(ab)p=apbp.

    Expand with the binomial theorem: the middle coefficients (pk)\binom pk(kp​), 0<k<p0 \lt k \lt p0<k<p, are divisible by ppp, and p⋅c=0p \cdot c = 0p⋅c=0 for every element ccc. The product rule holds in any commutative setting.

    More detail

    The divisibility of (pk)\binom pk(kp​) by ppp is the key lemma of Fermat's Little Theorem: ppp divides the numerator p!p!p! but not the denominator k!(p−k)!k!(p-k)!k!(p−k)!.

    So φ\varphiφ preserves sums and products, and also 000 and 111. Over F2\mathbb F_2F2​ it is squaring: (a+b)2=a2+b2(a + b)^2 = a^2 + b^2(a+b)2=a2+b2 because 2ab=02ab = 02ab=0.

  2. 2A symmetry

    φ\varphiφ is a bijection from the field to itself, so it is a symmetry (an automorphism) of the field.

    If ap=bpa^p = b^pap=bp then (a−b)p=0(a - b)^p = 0(a−b)p=0 by step 1, so a=ba = ba=b (no zero divisors). A one-to-one map from a finite set to itself is onto.

    More detail

    On an infinite field of characteristic ppp, φ\varphiφ can fail to be onto (on the field of rational functions Fp(t)\mathbb F_p(t)Fp​(t), nothing maps to ttt). Finiteness is what makes it a symmetry for free.

  3. 3What it fixes

    φ(a)=a\varphi(a) = aφ(a)=a exactly for the ppp elements 0,1,…,p−10, 1, \dots, p - 10,1,…,p−1 of Fp\mathbb F_pFp​.

    Those satisfy ap=aa^p = aap=a by Fermat's Little Theorem. And the fixed elements are roots of xp−xx^p - xxp−x, which has at most ppp roots, so there are no others.

    More detail

    The copy of Fp\mathbb F_pFp​ inside the field is {0,1,1+1,… }\{0, 1, 1 + 1, \dots\}{0,1,1+1,…}, the multiples of 111. Every symmetry fixes it, since it fixes 111 and preserves sums; Frobenius fixes nothing more.

  4. 4Exactly n different symmetries

    φn\varphi^nφn is the identity, but φk\varphi^kφk is not for 0<k<n0 \lt k \lt n0<k<n. So id,φ,…,φn−1\mathrm{id}, \varphi, \dots, \varphi^{n-1}id,φ,…,φn−1 are nnn different symmetries.

    φk(a)=apk\varphi^k(a) = a^{p^k}φk(a)=apk. Every aaa satisfies apn=aa^{p^n} = aapn=a (for a≠0a \ne 0a=0, apn−1=1a^{p^n - 1} = 1apn−1=1 by Lagrange). If φk\varphi^kφk were the identity with k<nk \lt nk<n, all pnp^npn elements would be roots of xpk−xx^{p^k} - xxpk−x, a polynomial of degree pk<pnp^k \lt p^npk<pn.

    More detail

    These are in fact all the symmetries: the Galois group of Fpn\mathbb F_{p^n}Fpn​ over Fp\mathbb F_pFp​ is cyclic of order nnn, generated by φ\varphiφ. (Sketch: a symmetry is determined by where it sends a primitive element α\alphaα, and it must send α\alphaα to a root of α\alphaα's minimal polynomial, which has degree nnn and therefore at most nnn roots.) The Galois theory destination builds on this case.

  5. 5Roots come in orbits

    If ggg has coefficients in Fp\mathbb F_pFp​ and g(β)=0g(\beta) = 0g(β)=0, then g(βp)=0g(\beta^p) = 0g(βp)=0. So the roots of ggg fall into whole orbits β,βp,βp2,…\beta, \beta^p, \beta^{p^2}, \dotsβ,βp,βp2,…

    Apply φ\varphiφ to g(β)=0g(\beta) = 0g(β)=0. It preserves sums and products and fixes the coefficients of ggg, so 0=φ(g(β))=g(φ(β))=g(βp)0 = \varphi(g(\beta)) = g(\varphi(\beta)) = g(\beta^p)0=φ(g(β))=g(φ(β))=g(βp).

    More detail

    Over the reals, the same argument with complex conjugation in place of φ\varphiφ shows that a real polynomial's non-real roots come in conjugate pairs.

    Consequence: the smallest polynomial over Fp\mathbb F_pFp​ with a given root β\betaβ (its minimal polynomial) must have the whole orbit as roots. In F16\mathbb F_{16}F16​ the orbit of α\alphaα has 444 elements, the orbit of α3\alpha^3α3 has 444, and the orbit of α5={α5,α10}\alpha^5 = \{\alpha^5, \alpha^{10}\}α5={α5,α10} only 222, so their minimal polynomials have degrees 444, 444 and 222: x4+x+1x^4 + x + 1x4+x+1, x4+x3+x2+x+1x^4 + x^3 + x^2 + x + 1x4+x3+x2+x+1 and x2+x+1x^2 + x + 1x2+x+1.

  6. 6The format strip of a QR codecited

    A QR code protects its 555 format bits (error-correction level and mask) with 101010 check bits, using g(x)=x10+x8+x5+x4+x2+x+1g(x) = x^{10} + x^8 + x^5 + x^4 + x^2 + x + 1g(x)=x10+x8+x5+x4+x2+x+1. Its roots in F16\mathbb F_{16}F16​ are the 101010 powers of α\alphaα in the orbits of 111, 333 and 555.

    BCH codes: Hocquenghem, 1959; Bose and Ray-Chaudhuri, 1960. QR format information: ISO/IEC 18004

    To correct 333 wrong bits, the BCH design asks for a polynomial over F2\mathbb F_2F2​ with the six consecutive roots α,α2,…,α6\alpha, \alpha^2, \dots, \alpha^6α,α2,…,α6. By step 5 it must then contain their whole orbits, {1,2,4,8}\{1, 2, 4, 8\}{1,2,4,8}, {3,6,12,9}\{3, 6, 12, 9\}{3,6,12,9} and {5,10}\{5, 10\}{5,10}: 101010 roots, so degree 101010, so 101010 check bits.

    More detail

    Multiply the three minimal polynomials from step 5: (x4+x+1)(x4+x3+x2+x+1)(x2+x+1)=x10+x8+x5+x4+x2+x+1.(x^4 + x + 1)(x^4 + x^3 + x^2 + x + 1)(x^2 + x + 1) = x^{10} + x^8 + x^5 + x^4 + x^2 + x + 1.(x4+x+1)(x4+x3+x2+x+1)(x2+x+1)=x10+x8+x5+x4+x2+x+1. The 555 format bits become the coefficients of x14,…,x10x^{14}, \dots, x^{10}x14,…,x10, and the check bits are the remainder that makes the whole 151515-bit word a multiple of ggg. The BCH bound (cited) says that a nonzero multiple of a polynomial with δ−1\delta - 1δ−1 consecutive roots α,…,αδ−1\alpha, \dots, \alpha^{\delta - 1}α,…,αδ−1 has at least δ\deltaδ nonzero coefficients. Here δ=7\delta = 7δ=7: any two format words differ in at least 777 bits (a computer confirms it for all 323232), so 333 misread bits are corrected.

    The strip is printed twice, around the corner squares, and read before anything else: the scanner needs the mask and the level to decode the rest.

Takeaway. One map, a↦apa \mapsto a^pa↦ap, generates every symmetry of a finite field. Roots of polynomials over Fp\mathbb F_pFp​ come in its orbits, which is why codes over F2\mathbb F_2F2​, like the QR format strip, are designed orbit by orbit.
