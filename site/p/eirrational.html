    More detail

    The idea: rational numbers can't be approximated too well by fractions with small denominators, but the partial sums of eee approximate it extremely well (error about 1/(q+1)!1/(q+1)!1/(q+1)!). Quantifying this tension is the heart of transcendence theory: Hermite (1873) proved eee is not even a root of any polynomial with integer coefficients, and Lindemann (1882) did the same for π\piπ.

Takeaway. Multiply a hypothetical fraction by the right factor to get an integer, then squeeze it strictly between consecutive integers.
